Purpose of the Project

In this project, I demonstrate how to physically install and configure a modern SOHO (Small Office/Home Office) Wi-Fi router. The primary driver for upgrading the existing router wasn't equipment failure, but rather the desire to leverage next-generation Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) technology, ultra-low latency capabilities, and advanced network management features to future-proof my home network.

Key Benefits of Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be)

Why upgrading to 802.11be delivers a paradigm shift in wireless performance.

Multi-Link Operation (MLO)

Allows a device to transmit and receive data simultaneously across multiple frequency bands (2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz) for drastically increased throughput.

Ultra-Low Latency

Cuts wireless latency down to sub-5 milliseconds—representing an 80%+ reduction compared to Wi-Fi 6 technology.

320 MHz Channel Width

Doubles maximum channel bandwidth from 160 MHz to 320 MHz on the 6 GHz spectrum, enabling ultra-wide data highways.

4K-QAM (4096-QAM)

Increases data modulation density from 1024-QAM to 4096-QAM, delivering 20% more data per symbol for higher peak speeds.

Preamble Puncturing & Multi-RUs

Allows the router to "slice around" channel interference. Instead of blocking an entire wide channel when interference is detected, it punctures out only the affected frequency portion while utilizing the rest.

Physical Installation & Mounting

Placement strategy and wall mounting workflow.

Placement Strategy & Demarcation Point

Mounted in the living room near the main door, which serves as the internet service provider's demarcation point. Crucial Rule: Avoid mounting near reflective surfaces (mirrors, metal) or directly next to high-voltage electrical wiring to prevent wireless signal degradation or RF interference.

1

Measure Mounting Holes

Measure the exact distance between the two integrated mounting keyholes located on the router chassis backplate.

Chassis backplate check
2

Install Wall Anchors & Screws

Mark the wall using a level line, insert drywall anchors, and drive 2 screws leaving appropriate clearance for the keyhole hooks.

Anchor installation
3

Mount & Connect Cables

Hang the router securely onto the screws. Plug in the power adapter and connect the WAN Ethernet cable from your modem into the 2.5G WAN port.

Power & WAN connected

Router Configuration & Setup

Choose between simple mobile app setup or advanced web administration GUI.

Recommended for beginners Quick step-by-step mobile pairing via QR code or automatic broadcast detection.
Step 1

Download App

Download the TP-Link Tether app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

Step 2

Power & Connect

Power on the router and wait for the front status LEDs to remain solid and stable.

Step 3

Scan QR Code

Open Tether, select 'Add Router', and scan the default Wi-Fi QR code printed on the bottom label. Then basically just following the instruction.

Advanced Management Interface Provides granular control over network security, channel width, MLO settings, and DHCP rules.
1

Connect to Default SSID

Connect your client PC or phone to the default factory Wi-Fi SSID and password printed on the router label.

2

Navigate to Gateway IP

Open your browser and navigate to http://192.168.0.1. Then, enter the default WIFI password (everytime you log it will ask for the password). You can also create a TP-Link ID for management, then next time you don't need to enter the WIFI password which is also more secure.

3

Configure Wireless Settings & Security

Navigate to the Wireless tab to update your network settings: Now you rename your WIFI, password as well as password security encryption type. If you want to hide your WIFI, you can just need to check "Hide SSID", but next time if a new device want to connect to your WIFI, so they have to enter the your WIFI manually. For other setting just leave it default.
